### T-077: Read formulas in Markdown by a rule that leaves sums of money alone
- **Intent:** [INT-0030](../../../intents/INT-0030-mathematics-and-code.md)
- **Touches:** `scripts/remark-math-dollars.mjs` (new), `astro.config.mjs`, `package.json` and `package-lock.json` (add `micromark-extension-math`, `mdast-util-math`), `src/lib/__tests__/remark-math-dollars.test.ts` (new)
- **Depends on:** (none)
- **Acceptance criterion:** INT-0030 AC1 (how a formula is written), AC4 (code is never a formula), AC5 (money), AC6 (nothing vanishes).
- **Success criterion (EARS):**
  - **WHEN** a paragraph holds a single `$`, then text, then a single `$`, with no space or line end directly after the first, no space or line end directly before the second, and no digit directly after the second, **THEN** the reader **SHALL** yield one inline formula whose value is the text between them, exactly as typed (a formula may run over a line end), wherever the paragraph stands: in a list item, a block quotation, an alert, a footnote, a table cell, a link's text or emphasis.
  - **WHEN** the `$` that would close a pair has a space or line end directly before it or a digit directly after it, or the opening `$` has a space or line end directly after it, **THEN** the opening `$` **SHALL** be left as text, and reading **SHALL** go on from the next character, so that a later pair in the same paragraph that meets the first clause is still a formula (`It costs $5 and $x$ is a number.` yields the one formula `x`).
  - **WHEN**, between an opening `$` and the next `$`, there stands a backtick, the two characters `](`, or the three characters `://`, or that next `$` has a backslash directly before it, **THEN** the opening `$` **SHALL** be left as text, and what follows **SHALL** be read as the Markdown it is: inline code stays inline code, `\$` stays a dollar sign, and a link or an address in angle brackets stays a link with its address unchanged.
  - **WHEN** a `$` has no later `$` in its paragraph, or is written `\$`, **THEN** it **SHALL** be text.
  - **WHEN** a line that begins a block, or stands directly under a paragraph that has no unclosed `$$` in it, holds `$$` and nothing else, and a later line holds `$$` and nothing else, **THEN** the reader **SHALL** yield one set-apart formula whose value is the lines between them exactly as typed, whatever marks those lines begin with (`+ c` is part of the formula, not a list).
  - **WHEN** a line begins with `$$` and holds anything after it, **THEN** it **SHALL NOT** open a fence: it is text, and a `$$…$$` formula that begins on it is read within its paragraph, over as many lines as it runs; **WHEN** a line that is only `$$` stands directly under a paragraph that has an unclosed `$$` in it, **THEN** it **SHALL** close that formula and **SHALL NOT** open a fence. In each of `$$\begin{aligned}` … `\end{aligned}$$`, `$$a = b` then `$$`, and `$$\begin{aligned}` … `\end{aligned}` then `$$`, the paragraph that follows the formula **SHALL** be a paragraph still.
  - **WHEN** a formula written `$$…$$` within a paragraph is a direct child of the paragraph and has its line or lines to itself (it begins the paragraph or follows a line end, and ends the paragraph or is followed by a line end), **THEN** it **SHALL** be a set-apart formula that is a sibling of paragraphs and not inside one, the text before and after it each becoming a paragraph of its own; **WHEN** `$$…$$` stands in the middle of a line, or inside a heading, a table cell, emphasis or a link, **THEN** it **SHALL** be an inline formula.
  - **WHEN** a `$$` fence is opened and no later line holds `$$` and nothing else, **THEN** the reader **SHALL** mark what the fence took as an unclosed fence, so that T-078 and T-079 show it as typed and never lay it out; `fenceClosed()` **SHALL** say of any set-apart formula whether its fence was closed.
  - **WHEN** a dollar sign stands inside inline code or a code block, **THEN** it **SHALL NOT** open or close a formula, and the code **SHALL** be unchanged.
  - **WHEN** a document holds no dollar sign, **THEN** its syntax tree with the plugin **SHALL** equal its tree without it.
  - **WHEN** the reader meets the cases INT-0030 records as limits, **THEN** it **SHALL** behave as recorded there: `Cells $B$2:$C$3 in a sheet.` yields the formula `2:`; `Sum $a+$b here.` yields `a+`; a table row `|$a|$b|` yields no formula; and `$$ a = b` followed by a line `+ c $$` yields no formula, a paragraph `$$ a = b` and a list.
  - **WHEN** `astro.config.mjs` is read, **THEN** its Markdown settings **SHALL** list this plugin beside `remarkAlerts`, with highlighting still off.
- **Notes:** the plugin reads formulas with the two packages `remark-math` is made of, `micromark-extension-math` and `mdast-util-math`, composed here so that Tome's three changes sit where they must; the `remark-math` package itself is therefore not installed. (1) The rule for single dollar signs is a construct tried before the package's (a later extension is tried first), as tried in `../sprint-research/math-trial.md` section 3 and widened after the first plan review: the package finds a closing sign in the raw characters ahead, before any of them has been read as Markdown, so the look ahead walks the same characters and refuses a pair whose closing sign belongs to something else (clause 3). (2) The package takes whatever follows an opening `$$` on its line as a label, then reads to a line that is only `$$` or to the end of the document; found in the second plan review, and confirmed, that `$$\begin{aligned}` … `\end{aligned}$$` then loses its first line and takes the rest of the chapter. So the package's fence construct is wrapped: a fence line must be bare, and a bare line does not open a fence under a paragraph that holds an odd number of `$$` (read from the paragraph's own tokens). (3) The set-apart step is a tree step: it replaces the paragraph, or divides it, and writes the set-apart node as the package writes it for fences (`pre > code.language-math.math-display`). Sixteen shapes of `$$` were tried with the draft before locking; all behave as the clauses say. An unclosed fence still takes the rest of the document, as an unclosed code fence does in any Markdown; clause 8 makes sure that is shown and named, not laid out as mathematics. The module exports the plugin, `mathSyntax()` (the micromark extensions, for T-079 and T-084, which parse without remark), `setApart()` (the tree step of clause 7 on its own, so that search sets apart exactly the formulas the page does) and `fenceClosed()`. Plain `.mjs` with JSDoc types, as `scripts/remark-alerts.mjs`.

### T-078: Lay each formula out as MathML when the library is built
- **Intent:** [INT-0030](../../../intents/INT-0030-mathematics-and-code.md)
- **Touches:** `scripts/rehype-math.mjs` (new), `astro.config.mjs`, `package.json` and `package-lock.json` (add `temml`, `hast-util-from-html`, `hast-util-to-string`; `rehype-raw` as a development dependency for the test), `fixtures/proofs/` (new: `1-proof.md`, `2-more.md`, the two pages of formulas this task's tests, T-080's and T-081's gate all read), `src/lib/__tests__/rehype-math.test.ts` (new)
- **Depends on:** T-077
- **Acceptance criterion:** INT-0030 AC1 (laid out, no script, no network), AC2 (the constructs, equation numbers), AC4 (code untouched), AC6 (an unreadable formula).
- **Success criterion (EARS):**
  - **WHEN** a chapter holds an inline formula, **THEN** its HTML **SHALL** hold a `math` element in its place with no `display="block"`, and no `code` element of class `language-math` **SHALL** remain.
  - **WHEN** a chapter holds a set-apart formula, written with `$$` or as a fenced code block in the language `math`, **THEN** its HTML **SHALL** hold a `div` of class `tome-math` whose only child is a `math` element with `display="block"`, in place of the `pre`; and after Astro's own later steps (`rehype-raw` among them) that `div` **SHALL NOT** be inside a `p`, and no empty `p` **SHALL** stand beside it.
  - **WHEN** any formula is laid out, **THEN** the result **SHALL** hold no `semantics` or `annotation` element, no `script` or `a` element, no `id`, no attribute whose name begins `on`, and no `href` or `src`.
  - **WHEN** a set-apart formula is written by Temml as a table with the style `width:100%` (a numbered or tagged environment), **THEN** that table's width **SHALL** be `100cqi` in the chapter's HTML and no formula table **SHALL** keep a percent width.
  - **WHEN** a page's formulas ask for automatic numbers (`equation`, `align`, `gather` and the like), **THEN** each numbered row **SHALL** hold the text `(n)`, with n counting from 1 in the order of the page, and a second page **SHALL** start again at 1; a starred environment and a row marked `\nonumber` or `\notag` **SHALL** hold no number and use none up; a row given `\tag{…}` **SHALL** show that tag and use no number up; no empty numbering placeholder (`tml-eqn`) **SHALL** remain; and the cell that holds a row's number or tag **SHALL** carry Temml's class for a cell set to the right (`tml-right`).
  - **WHEN** each of a fraction, a root with an index, a sum with limits, a matrix in round brackets, `cases`, an aligned derivation and a numbered equation is laid out, **THEN** the result **SHALL** hold the MathML element that construct needs (`mfrac`; `mroot`; `munderover` or `msubsup` with the summation sign; `mtable` between stretchy brackets; `mtable` after a stretchy brace; an `mtable` whose rows each hold the same number of cells; an `mtable` holding the number).
  - **WHEN** an inline formula's laid-out text is longer than 30 characters, **THEN** its `math` element **SHALL** be the only child of a `span` of class `tome-math-long`; a shorter one **SHALL** have no wrapper.
  - **WHEN** Temml cannot read a formula, or the formula is what a `$$` fence took that was never closed (T-077), **THEN** the chapter **SHALL** hold, in its place, a `code` element of class `tome-math-error` whose text is `asTyped()` of the formula (its text between `$…$` when within a line, `$$…$$` when set apart); the plugin **SHALL** record one message on the file that holds the formula's first 60 characters with white space collapsed; it **SHALL** print at least one line that holds the chapter file's path and those characters; and it **SHALL NOT** throw, so the rest of the chapter and of the build goes on.
  - **WHEN** a formula uses a command Temml refuses (`\href`, `\url`, `\includegraphics`, `\class`, `\data`) or one that would leave a link for a script to fill (`\ref`, `\eqref`), **THEN** it **SHALL** be handled as an unreadable formula, and no link, image or attribute **SHALL** come from it; **WHEN** a formula holds `\label{…}`, **THEN** it **SHALL** be laid out and no `id` **SHALL** come from it.
  - **WHEN** one formula defines a command (`\def`) and a later formula on the same page uses it, **THEN** the later formula **SHALL** be handled as unreadable (each formula is read on its own), and the first **SHALL** be laid out.
  - **WHEN** a chapter holds inline code, a code block in any language other than `math`, or a `code` element of another class, **THEN** each **SHALL** be unchanged; **WHEN** a chapter holds no formula, **THEN** its tree **SHALL** be unchanged.
  - **WHEN** the module's exported functions are called, **THEN** `readable(tex, display)` **SHALL** be true exactly when the plugin would lay that formula out; `formulaText(tex, display)` **SHALL** be the text of the `math` element the plugin writes for that formula, within a line or (when `display` is true) set apart, with nothing added between its parts and without the cell that holds a row's number or tag, and `null` when unreadable (a numbered environment or `\tag`, which Temml reads only set apart, is `null` within a line and text when set apart); `asTyped(tex, display)` **SHALL** be the text the plugin puts in the error element; and `titleText(tex)` **SHALL** be the formula as one line of plain text that keeps what the layout says, a root with its sign (`\sqrt{2}` is `√2`), a fraction with a stroke (`\frac{a+b}{c}` is `(a+b)/c`), a power or an index raised or lowered (`x^2` is `x²`, `a_i` is `aᵢ`, and `^` or `_` before it where Unicode has no such character), a relation with a space either side (`E = mc^2` is `E = mc²`), and every part of a root, a fraction, a power or an index (radicand, numerator, denominator, base, and an exponent or index written with `^` or `_`) in round brackets unless it is one letter, one symbol or one number, or stands in brackets already, and a number that directly follows a root or a fraction parted from it by `·`, so that what a root, a fraction or a power holds is never run together with what stands beside it (`\sqrt{2x}` is `√(2x)` and `\sqrt{2}x` is `√2x`; `\sqrt{2}3` is `√2·3` and `\sqrt{23}` is `√23`; `\frac{1}{2x}` is `1/(2x)`; `{a+b}^2` is `(a+b)²`; `e^{i\pi}` is `e^(iπ)`), and **SHALL** be `null` for a formula that is unreadable or whose layout has an element with no such form (a table, an accent, a limit above or below).
  - **WHEN** `astro.config.mjs` is read, **THEN** its Markdown settings **SHALL** list this plugin under `rehypePlugins`.
- **Notes:** follows `scripts/remark-alerts.mjs`. Temml's `renderToString(tex, { displayMode, throwOnError: true })`, with `annotate` and `trust` left off, and for a formula within a line its `wrap: 'tex'` option, which lets Chrome's engine break a long formula after a relation or an operator. The string is parsed into real elements with `hast-util-from-html` (a fragment), not passed through as raw HTML. Astro runs a user's rehype plugins after `remark-rehype` and before its heading collector and `rehype-raw` (`node_modules/@astrojs/markdown-remark/dist/index.js`), so the collector reads the formula's own text. The three WebKit corrections and their evidence: `../sprint-research/math-trial.md` section 4. Tried before locking: `\ref` and `\eqref` come out as an empty `a` of class `tml-ref`; `\label` writes an `id` on the row; `\href` and `\url` throw; a `\def` does not reach the next call. `rehype-math.test.ts` runs the real `rehypeHeadingIds` and `rehype-raw` after the plugin, as Astro does.

### T-079: Give search, and the rail, the text and the heading address of a formula
- **Intent:** [INT-0030](../../../intents/INT-0030-mathematics-and-code.md)
- **Touches:** `src/lib/search-index.ts`, `src/lib/__tests__/search-index.test.ts`, `src/lib/search.ts`, `src/lib/__tests__/search.test.ts`, `src/pages/[...slug].astro`
- **Depends on:** T-077, T-078
- **Acceptance criterion:** INT-0030 AC7 (search; a heading in plain-text places), AC5 (search and the page agree about money); INT-0008's boundary.
- **Success criterion (EARS):**
  - **WHEN** a chapter holds a formula Temml reads, written with dollar signs or as a fenced block in the language `math`, **THEN** its search text **SHALL** hold `formulaText()` of it (read set apart when it is set apart, so a numbered environment is found by what it shows and not by `begin`; a row's number or tag is no part of it), less the invisible characters Temml writes between a function and its argument, as a word of its own (`$E = mc^2$` is found as `E=mc2`) and **SHALL NOT** hold its dollar signs or its LaTeX commands, but for the next clause.
  - **WHEN** a formula's source names a symbol by a command whose whole output is one character outside ASCII (`\alpha`, `\le`, `\infty`, `\sum`), **THEN** the search text **SHALL** also hold that command's name as a word (`alpha`), so a formula of Greek letters and signs can still be found by typing; a command that builds a structure (`\frac`, `\sqrt`, `\begin`) **SHALL NOT** add a word.
  - **WHEN** a chapter holds a formula Temml cannot read, or a `$$` fence that was never closed, **THEN** its search text **SHALL** hold `asTyped()` of it, as the page shows it.
  - **WHEN** a line outside a table holds dollar signs that the rule of T-077 leaves as text, **THEN** the search text **SHALL** hold that line as typed; this **SHALL** hold for every line of T-077's tests that yields no formula, the ones with inline code, `\$` and a link's address among them.
  - **WHEN** a heading holds a formula, **THEN** the heading's slug in the search index **SHALL** equal the `id` that Astro's own heading collector gives the same heading after T-078's plugin has run, for a readable formula, an unreadable one, and two headings that hold the same formula; and the heading's text in the index **SHALL** hold `titleText()` of the formula (`asTyped()` where that is `null`), so a result reads `The E = mc² law`.
  - **WHEN** a chapter's page is built, **THEN** the "on this page" rail's entry for a heading that holds a formula **SHALL** read as the search index's text for that heading, its link unchanged; an entry for a heading with no formula **SHALL** read as before, whatever else it holds (a heading with a line break in it reads in the rail as it did). `formulaHeadings(raw)` **SHALL** give the text for exactly the headings that hold a formula, by slug.
  - **WHEN** a chapter holds no dollar sign and no fenced block in the language `math`, **THEN** its search text and headings **SHALL** be exactly what they were before this task (every existing case of `search-index.test.ts` passes unedited).
  - **WHEN** a formula's source stands in inline code, or in a code block in another language, **THEN** the search text **SHALL** treat it as code, as before.
  - **WHEN** text is split into words for search, in an indexed chapter or in what a reader types, **THEN** a raised or lowered character **SHALL** count as the plain one (`mc²` is the word `mc2`, `aᵢ` is `ai`), so a heading shown as `The E = mc² law` is found, and opened at its section, by typing `mc2`; all other text **SHALL** be split exactly as before (every existing case of `search.test.ts` passes unedited).
- **Notes:** `parseChapter()` calls `fromMarkdown` with `mathSyntax()` from T-077 and `mdast-util-math`'s tree extension, then runs T-077's `setApart()` on the tree, so a `$$…$$` formula the page sets apart is read set apart here too. An inline formula is a run of its own among the gathered parts; a set-apart formula stands between separators. The slug is made from `formulaText()` unaltered, because the page's collector reads the same characters; only the search text drops the invisible ones. Search splits text into runs of `a` to `z` and digits (`src/lib/search.ts`), so without the second clause `$\alpha + \beta$`, found today by typing `alpha`, would be found by nothing. The rail takes its text from Astro's collector, which flattens `x^2` to `x2`; the page now asks `formulaHeadings()`, a pure function beside `parseChapter()`, for the headings of the chapter that hold a formula (from the module's `rawContent()`), and uses its text for those slugs only, because the index's text for other headings already differs from the collector's in small ways the rail must not inherit. The fold in the last clause is a table of the raised and lowered digits, signs and letters, applied in `tokenize()`; it is not a general normalization, so accented letters are split as they always were. Search has never read tables as the page does (`fromMarkdown` without the table extension), so the fourth clause speaks of lines outside a table; that limit is in INT-0030's Consequences. T-081's gate compares slugs and reads the rail on a built site.

### T-082: Carry a Word document's equations over as LaTeX notation
- **Intent:** [INT-0030](../../../intents/INT-0030-mathematics-and-code.md)
- **Touches:** `scripts/omml-to-latex.mjs` (new), `scripts/docx-to-markdown.mjs`, `fixtures/word/paper/word/document.xml`, `package.json` and `package-lock.json` (add `@xmldom/xmldom`, already installed through the Word reader), `scripts/check-documents.mjs`, `e2e/documents.spec.ts`, `src/lib/__tests__/omml-to-latex.test.ts` (new), `src/lib/__tests__/docx-to-markdown.test.ts`, `src/lib/__tests__/document-stage.test.ts`, `src/lib/__tests__/load-books.test.ts`
- **Depends on:** T-077, T-078
- **Acceptance criterion:** INT-0030 AC3, AC5 (a dollar sign typed in a Word document); INT-0029's boundary (every other rule of the converter stands).
- **Success criterion (EARS):**
  - **WHEN** `ommlToLatex()` is given a Word equation built of the elements it knows, **THEN** it **SHALL** return LaTeX for it: a fraction as `\frac{…}{…}`, a slanted or in-line one with `/`, and one with no bar as a stack; a root as `\sqrt{…}` and with a degree as `\sqrt[n]{…}`; a sum, product or integral with its limits as the sign with `_{…}^{…}` (an integral when the sign is not named, a limit left out when the document hides it); a superscript, a subscript, both, and a pre-script; brackets as `\left…\right` with the document's own characters, round when none is named, and `.` on a side the document leaves empty; a matrix inside round, square or curly brackets or bars as `pmatrix`, `bmatrix`, `Bmatrix`, `vmatrix`, and with none as `matrix`; lines aligned at `&` as `aligned`, and inside a lone left brace as `cases`; a named function as its command (`\sin`, `\lim`) when LaTeX has one and as `\operatorname{…}` when not; a limit under a word as `\lim_{…}` or `\underset`; an accent and a bar as `\hat`, `\tilde`, `\vec`, `\dot`, `\ddot`, `\bar`, `\overline`, `\underline`; a run marked bold, double-struck, script or fraktur as `\mathbf`, `\mathbb`, `\mathcal`, `\mathfrak`; text marked as plain words as `\text{…}`; several equations in one paragraph as lines of one `gathered`; and it **SHALL** pass over what Word adds that changes nothing to lay out (a run's font, the properties of a control character, a bookmark, a mark of italic on a run that is italic anyway).
  - **WHEN** an equation's text holds a letter, digit or symbol (`α`, `≤`, `∑`, `∞`, `→`), **THEN** it **SHALL** be written as itself; **WHEN** it holds a character LaTeX gives a meaning (`\`, `{`, `}`, `#`, `%`, `&` outside aligned lines, `_`, `^`, `~`, `$`), **THEN** it **SHALL** be written so that it shows as that character.
  - **WHEN** an equation holds an element of Word's equation markup that the converter has no rule for, or an element of another kind that itself holds part of the equation (a tracked insertion or deletion, a link, a content control), or a bracket, accent, grouping mark, kind of fraction or style of run it has no rule for, or yields LaTeX that `readable()` (T-078) refuses, **THEN** `ommlToLatex()` **SHALL** return nothing for the whole equation, never part of it.
  - **WHEN** `convertDocx()` meets an equation the converter returns LaTeX for, **THEN** the Markdown **SHALL** hold that LaTeX between dollar signs where the equation stood, with the text either side kept: `$…$` for an equation within a line, in a heading or in a table cell; `$$…$$` on one line as a paragraph of its own for an equation that is a paragraph of its own, whether that paragraph stands in the body, in a list item, in a quotation or in a note; and the Markdown's own escaping **SHALL NOT** have changed a character of the LaTeX.
  - **WHEN** the Markdown `convertDocx()` wrote is read by the rule of T-077, **THEN** every carried-over equation **SHALL** be read as a formula with exactly the LaTeX the converter returned (so an equation directly followed by a digit, or one whose LaTeX holds a dollar sign, a backtick, `](` or `://`, or ends in a dollar sign, is written so that it still is one); a set-apart one **SHALL** be set apart, inside its list item, quotation or note when it stood in one; and a table that holds an equation **SHALL** still be a table with the same cells.
  - **WHEN** a Word document's own text holds a dollar sign, **THEN** the Markdown **SHALL** hold it as `\$`, so that read by the rule of T-077 the document's text yields no formula and each such sign reads as a dollar sign.
  - **WHEN** a Word document's title holds an equation, **THEN** the title `convertDocx()` returns **SHALL** hold `titleText()` of it where it stood, the equation between dollar signs as the chapter has it when `titleText()` is `null`, or `[equation not shown]` when the converter returns nothing; and never the place-holder the converter uses on the way.
  - **WHEN** `convertDocx()` meets an equation the converter returns nothing for, **THEN** the chapter **SHALL** read `[equation not shown]` in its place as before, as literal text that can never form a link, whatever follows it; and the list of what was left out **SHALL** count only those equations, holding no `equation` entry when every one was carried over.

- **Notes:** `markUnreadable()` in `docx-to-markdown.mjs` already finds each equation in the document's XML before the Word reader sees it. A set-apart equation is written on one line (`$$…$$` alone in its paragraph), which T-077 sets apart wherever the paragraph stands; a three-line form would lose a list's indent or a quotation's mark on its second line. The Markdown writer is given `$` as a character to escape in running text, so the only bare dollar signs in the result are the ones the converter put there. In a table cell `|` is written `\vert`.
