# On Reading

To read is to run a small machine by hand. The eye is the tape head; the line is
the tape; meaning is the halting state you hope to reach before the page ends.

A good page asks little of the reader and returns much. It keeps a steady measure,
lets the paragraphs breathe, and never shouts. The reader supplies attention; the
page supplies form. Between them, a book happens.

- Read slowly enough to hear the sentences.
- Mark the margins; a book you have argued with is a book you have read.
- Return to the hard passages — they were hard for a reason.
