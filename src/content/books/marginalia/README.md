# Colophon

*Marginalia* is the second tome in this bibliotheca — a short companion to the
sample **Tome**, kept here so the library has more than one shelf to browse.

These are notes written in the margin: a few short reflections on reading, on
machines, and on the quiet craft of setting words on a page. Nothing here needs
to be read in order — turn to whichever note catches the eye.

> The margin is where the reader answers the book.
